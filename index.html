About Me

I'm a cybersecurity researcher with over a decade of experience spanning applied research, software engineering and European R&D projects. My work focuses on cyber threat intelligence, threat analysis, secure information sharing, OSINT-driven enrichment and AI-assisted cybersecurity. I combine academic depth — a PhD in multimedia forensics and computer vision — with hands-on engineering across Python, MISP, machine learning and cloud-native tooling. I've delivered technical work packages, prototypes, publications and proposals in multi-partner international consortia, and I teach in a Master's programme in Cybersecurity.
